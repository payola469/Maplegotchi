# Maple's room

The room has a bed for sleeping, a sofa for resting, a writing desk, a separate computer desk for looking at the server, a bookshelf, a window with a plant beside it, and an open area of floor to walk around on.

## Moving around

Maple walks to a piece of furniture before doing anything there. Reading happens at the bookshelf, writing at the writing desk, and checking the server at the computer desk. Thinking happens by the window, and walking or idling happens in the open area.
